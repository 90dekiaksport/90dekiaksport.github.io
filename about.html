Our Mission

Our mission is to provide every Ethiopian, whether at home or in the diaspora, with instant access to their favorite local radio stations through a reliable, fast, and high-quality streaming platform.

Who We Are

We are a dedicated team of Android developers and radio enthusiasts based in Addis Ababa. We noticed that many streaming apps were either too slow or filled with intrusive interruptions. We built Ethiopian Radio to solve that—focusing on clean UI and high-performance streaming.

Why Choose Our App?

  • Optimized for Low Data: We know internet speeds vary, so our app is built to stream smoothly even on 3G or throttled connections.
  • Community Driven: We constantly update our station list based on user requests from our community.
  • Modern Technology: Built with the latest Android development standards for stability and speed.
