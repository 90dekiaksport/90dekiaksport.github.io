This Privacy Policy explains how Ethiopian Radio ("the app") handles information. By using the app, you agree to these terms.

1. Information Collection & Use

We do not collect personal identifiers like your name, email, or phone number. However, we use third-party tools (Firebase and AdMob) that collect technical data to keep the app running smoothly.

  • Streaming Data: Your IP address is sent to radio stream providers to facilitate the connection but is not stored by us.
  • Device Info: We track device models and OS versions to fix crashes.

2. Advertising & Personalized Ads

We use Google AdMob to serve advertisements. AdMob uses unique identifiers (like the Android Advertising ID) to show you relevant ads.

EEA & UK Users: In compliance with GDPR, we use a Google-certified Consent Management Platform (CMP). You will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown. You can revoke or change your consent at any time within the app's settings menu.

Learn how Google manages data in its ad products.

3. Third-Party Services

Service Purpose
Firebase Content updates and crash reporting.
Google AdMob Displaying relevant ads to users.

4. Data Security & Retention

Since we do not collect personal data, we do not store personal information on our servers. Technical data collected by Google is stored according to their standard retention policies (typically 9-18 months).
